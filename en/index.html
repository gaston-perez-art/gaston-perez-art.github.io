03 · Process

How I work

  1. 01People first.Every project starts with someone specific and their problem: a teacher, a coach, a relative, a group of friends.
  2. 02Decisions, in writing.Every product has its trade-offs documented: what I chose, why, and what I left out.
  3. 03The minimum that validates.Zero cost until there is demand. A backend when it is needed, not before.
  4. 04AI every day.I build with Claude Code, v0 and Cursor. What to build and what to leave out is my call.
