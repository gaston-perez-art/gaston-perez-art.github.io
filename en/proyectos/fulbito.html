A 5-a-side table that does not lie

Family tournament, Saturdays at 6 PM, 12 match days. Teams are reshuffled every week, so the unit that competes is the player: points stick to the person.

Decision

Goals are not counted per player

The score belongs to the team and to everyone who was on the pitch. In 5-a-side nobody can keep track of who scored each goal, and a sheet nobody can truly fill in is a sheet that lies. Goals only break ties.

Decision

Only those who played score

Before playing, teams are drawn from whoever shows up. Afterwards, only players who went through the draw can be entered. An empty score is not a zero: the save button stays locked until both are in.

Decision

The guest competes like everyone else

Whoever came for a single Saturday paid the fee like everyone, so they join the table with their one match day and sit at the bottom, without messing up the top.
