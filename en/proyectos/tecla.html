Tecla · 2026 · Live · validating

Validating demand before investing a single peso

Tecla has two units: IT repair and support for teachers in the Buenos Aires province school system. The support unit serves about 10 clients a month. The business plan is my MBA thesis.

Two businesses, one landing page

A switch at the top separates the two units. The site does not sell or charge: it presents, measures and collects leads.

Decision

Zero-cost serverless

Hosting on GitHub Pages, DNS on Cloudflare, leads to Google Sheets through a form and analytics in GA4. There is no server to maintain. When the teacher product needs to store the user’s work, that is when a backend is justified.

The trade-offNo accounts or own data for now. That is right while the question is whether there is demand.

From thesis to market

Tecla’s business plan is the final project of my MBA at Universidad de Palermo. The site is the market validation of that plan.
