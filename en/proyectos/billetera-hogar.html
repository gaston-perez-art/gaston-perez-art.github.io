Billetera Hogar · 2026 · In use at home

A wallet for two that replaced the spreadsheet

A wallet-style web app so a couple can log household expenses from their phones and see, every month, how much each one should chip in and how much is really left.

Try the demo ↗Code ↗

The demo uses made-up amounts and stores everything in your browser, with no login. You can log an expense, mark a fixed payment as paid and watch the split change. "Volver a los datos de demo" in Settings (Ajustes) resets everything. The app is in Spanish.

  • Upcoming payments: how many, how much and when the first is due

How I thought it through

The problem

Household expenses lived in a Google Sheets spreadsheet. It worked, but logging an expense from a phone was slow and error-prone: wrong row, wrong sheet, "who paid" picked by hand on every row. And marking a fixed payment as paid did not record it as an expense, so it had to be entered twice, and it got forgotten.

Log an expense in three taps

Amount, category and detail. The date is today and the payer is whoever is signed in, unless changed. If logging is costly, it does not get done, and an account with gaps is useless for deciding anything.

Marking as paid creates the expense

The month’s fixed payments are sorted by due date. Marking one as paid creates the expense in its category; unmarking deletes it. One action, one record.

Each one pays in proportion to what they earn

The split is not fifty-fifty. The app calculates how much each person should chip in based on their share of total income, compares it with what they actually paid and shows the difference. The number that counts is the end-of-month one.

Closed categories, with a cap

No free text for categories: in the spreadsheet that was why some expenses never added up in the month. Each category has a monthly cap and the app shows what is left in green and any overspend in red.

How the split is calculated

ConceptCalculation
Should chip inTheir share of total income × expected spending for the month
PaidExpenses this month paid by that person
DifferenceWhat they really have left minus what they should have left

What I built

  • Month home: money in, spent, pending payments and what is really left.
  • Per-person login and real-time sync: what one logs, the other sees instantly.
  • Installable on the phone as an app, with a reminder after half a day without logging anything.
  • An eye icon to hide amounts and an export to keep using the spreadsheet when needed.
  • A demo mode with fictional data in the browser, the same one you can try here.
