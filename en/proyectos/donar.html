donAR · 2026 · Live web demo

Fundraising where trust is the product

You post your need, a curator verifies it before it goes live, you donate directly to the person and you see where every peso went. I took it from the idea to a POC with real data: product, design, code, legal and go-to-market.

Try the demo ↗Code ↗

The demo runs on sample data stored in your browser, with no account. You can donate, confirm a transfer as a cause owner and approve causes as a curator. The app is in Spanish. In the real app, identity is mandatory.

What you can do

Fundraiser

  • Create a cause with story, goal, deadline and photos
  • Verify identity with ID, selfie and documents
  • Cause dashboard: raised, donations and sharing
  • Confirm each transfer received
  • Close with a message and thank every donor

Donor

  • Feed of verified causes
  • Donate by bank transfer with a receipt
  • See where the money went in each cause
  • Profile with history, level and badges
  • Monthly ranking with a mini-profile of each donor

Curator

  • Queue of causes under review
  • Approve, reject or request more information after reviewing the evidence

Automatic rules

  • Transfers auto-confirm after 48 hours, with a visible countdown
  • Causes close when they reach the goal or the deadline passes
  • Closing report: how much, how many people and how fast
  • Minimum amount to ask: ARS 5,000

How it is built

  1. AppReact Native with Expo and TypeScript: one codebase for iOS, Android and web
  2. DataSupabase: Postgres with RLS, email login and private Storage for identity evidence
  3. PaymentsDirect transfer with receipt. Mercado Pago integrated and switched off behind a flag until split payments and legal sign-off exist
  4. No own serverClosures and auto-confirmations run on the client: zero infrastructure to maintain
  5. DemoWeb build with a fake database in the browser, published on GitHub Pages

How I thought it through

The problem

Solidarity between strangers already works at scale, but with no infrastructure to support it. Viral fundraisers prove two things at once: people give money when a cause feels fair, and trust is the weak point. In 2023 Argentina’s corporate regulator declared the trust behind one of the country’s best-known fundraisers irregular. The money arrived; the way it was managed came under suspicion.

The platforms in Argentina work with NGOs, not with people. GoFundMe allows individual fundraisers, but it neither verifies them nor turns traceability into a product. I found none that combined three things: person-to-person fundraising, a trust layer and a reason to come back.

Connect without holding the money

Money goes directly from donor to recipient and never passes through the app. Central bank regulation is triggered by holding third-party balances, not by connecting people. No custody: less friction, zero third-party funds and a clean regulatory frame. I backed it with a money-laundering risk analysis and a sharp question for the lawyer.

The costThe platform can never technically prove a transfer arrived, because it never sees it. Accepting that was the design premise.

Only confirmed money counts

A donation with an uploaded receipt but no confirmation stays pending: it does not add to the goal, earn points or trigger recognition. It only counts once the recipient confirms it arrived. The recipient is the only one who sees the money come in and gains nothing by confirming what they did not receive. If they do not respond within 48 hours, it confirms itself, so nobody gets stuck.

Gamification through recognition, no prizes

Levels, badges and a monthly ranking of top helpers, at no monetary cost. If the reward is material, you attract donors who want the prize, not the cause. And it lets you measure what matters: whether people come back for the cause.

What the MVP validates

HypothesisMetric

If all three show signal at small scale, there is a product. If not, the lesson was cheap.

What I built

  • A feed of verified causes and a curation panel, where a human reviews the evidence before publishing.
  • Donation by bank transfer with receipt, recipient confirmation and auto-confirmation after 48 hours.
  • Monthly ranking, badges and onboarding tours.
  • Mercado Pago integrated and switched off: it turns on once split payments and legal sign-off exist.
  • A web version from the same codebase, with a demo mode that swaps the database for sample data so anyone can try it without installing anything.
