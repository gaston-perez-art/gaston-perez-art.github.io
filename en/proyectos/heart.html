Heart · 2026 · Used every day

Ten pills a day, for someone who did not want to learn a new app

A PWA so an elderly relative can mark each dose with one tap, and so the family can see from their phones how the day is going, which medication is running out, and leave him a message.

Try the demo ↗

The demo uses made-up data and stores everything in your browser. Use the bar above the tabs to switch between the patient view and the family view. The app is in Spanish. The code is private because the real app handles health data.

What you can do

Patient

  • Today: the day’s blocks with their pills; one tap marks the dose and stores the real time
  • Blood pressure log with its own keypad, without interpreting the number
  • One message a day from family and friends
  • The day’s closing card when all ten doses are done

Family

  • Live dashboard: how many he took, the status of each block and the pill that is running out
  • Stock per medication with days left; adding a box shows the math before saving
  • The week as a grid, like the printed chart
  • Moderate the message wall and share its public link

Accessibility

  • Text size that scales the whole screen together
  • Four themes, light and dark
  • Works offline and syncs when the signal returns
  • Updates notify, they never install themselves

How it is built

  1. AppReact and Vite as an installable PWA, with a service worker and self-hosted fonts to work offline
  2. DataSupabase with RLS: only the patient can mark a dose, enforced by the database and the UI
  3. IntegrityStock is moved by triggers and functions: never written directly, every movement logged
  4. Offline firstLocal queue with retries and an operation ID so nothing is counted twice
  5. DemoSeparate build with made-up data stored in the browser

How I thought it through

What it does

He opens the app and sees Today: the four blocks of the day with their medications. One tap marks the dose and stores the real time. The family sees the dashboard: how many he took, the status of each block and the medication that is running out. It updates on its own when he marks something.

When all ten are done, the card turns into the day’s closing: a beating heart with a monitor trace and the phrase he says every morning.

Design decisions

The day starts at 4 AM, not midnight

If he takes his sleeping pill at 00:30, it counts for the previous day.

The app records, it does not interpret

The blood pressure log has no color ranges or trends. Interpreting a number is the doctor’s job. The history is shared over WhatsApp in one tap.

Nothing changes under his fingers

Unmarking takes two taps. Updates notify, they do not install themselves. Everything is in rem: one selector enlarges text, buttons and spacing together, and the screen never overflows. No web fonts: Roboto is already on the phone and the app works offline.

Only the patient marks a dose

Enforced by database permissions and by the interface. If anyone can tick, the log stops telling what happened.

Stock that counts itself down

Each dose subtracts according to the dosage, and cards are sorted by urgency. Adding a box shows the math before saving: "38 tablets, enough for 38 days". An empty stock and a stock nobody entered are two different things, and the app treats them differently.
